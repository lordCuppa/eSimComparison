## 🏆 Top Recommendations
**Best Overall Value**: Campus SIM. For an exchange student, having a local 090/080/070 number is a "life-changer" for setting up bank accounts or receiving calls from the university.

**Best Secondary SIM**: Roamless. Use it as a backup. Since the credit doesn't expire, you can keep $10-20 on it for emergencies if your main provider has a blackout.

**Best for Tokyo/Osaka**: Ubigi. Their 5G integration with NTT Docomo is superior in dense urban areas, offering very low latency.

## ⚠️ Important Price Factors
**Activation Fees**: Long-term providers (Campus SIM/Sakura) often have a one-time fee (~3,000 JPY), which is included in the totals above.

**Top-ups**: Travel eSIMs are often calculated in 30-day cycles. For 144 days, you will need to purchase 5 cycles.

**Consumption**: 20 GB/month (Campus SIM) is usually enough for daily commuting and social media, but not for replacing home Wi-Fi for gaming or 4K streaming.

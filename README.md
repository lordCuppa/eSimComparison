For my 144 days in Japan, I have decided to trust the Campus SIM.

Why? Because as a student, budget matters — and you simply can't beat their price-to-data value. Beyond the numbers, there is a "peace of mind" factor in using a local Japanese provider; I'm looking forward to testing their network stability in the field!

For me the best decision will be (updated in the future) to take the specific student eSim. This decision is based on the **price/GB** value and the hope that a japanese provider **will work flawless** in Japan.

## 🔍 Quick Decision Matrix
If you are unsure which one to pick based on the monthly cost, use this guide:

<ul>
  <li><b>The "Thrifty Scholar" (€18 - €20 / Month)</b>: Go with Campus SIM. It’s the unbeatable price leader for students and the only one in this price bracket that gives you a phone number.</li>

  <li><b>The "Digital Nomad" (€22 - €28 / Month)</b>: Ubigi or Jetpac are your best bets. You get high-speed 5G data without the paperwork of a residency-based contract.</li>

  <li><b>The "Streaming Addict" (€40+ / Month)</b>: If you watch YouTube or Twitch on the train every day, Holafly's unlimited plan is worth the premium to avoid "data anxiety."</li>
</ul>

## 💡 Good to Know: The Student Discount
The reason Campus SIM is significantly cheaper per month is that it is a subsidized offer specifically for the exchange student community. While travel eSIMs (like Airalo) include a "convenience tax" for instant activation, Campus SIM rewards you for the slightly longer application process with a much lower monthly rate.

***Note**: Monthly averages are calculated based on the total cost of a 144-day stay divided by 4.8 months.*

## 🏆 Top Recommendations
**Best Overall Value**: Campus SIM. For an exchange student, having a local 090/080/070 number is a "life-changer" for setting up bank accounts or receiving calls from the university.

**Best Secondary SIM**: Roamless. Use it as a backup. Since the credit doesn't expire, you can keep $10-20 on it for emergencies if your main provider has a blackout.

**Best for Tokyo/Osaka**: Ubigi. Their 5G integration with NTT Docomo is superior in dense urban areas, offering very low latency.

## ⚠️ Important Price Factors
**Activation Fees**: Long-term providers (Campus SIM/Sakura) often have a one-time fee (~3,000 JPY), which is included in the totals above.

**Top-ups**: Travel eSIMs are often calculated in 30-day cycles. For 144 days, you will need to purchase 5 cycles.

**Consumption**: 20 GB/month (Campus SIM) is usually enough for daily commuting and social media, but not for replacing home Wi-Fi for gaming or 4K streaming.
